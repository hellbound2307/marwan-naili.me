Home Disclosure

I measure and analyse exposure, analyse malware and exfiltration channels, and build reproducible data tooling. All work is either measurement-only, or authorized, or conducted on malware in isolation. I publish the method alongside the finding — a result you cannot reproduce is an anecdote.

Focus areas

  • Exposure measurementInternet-facing asset and service census: protocol fingerprinting, misconfiguration triage, ICS/SCADA enumeration. Measurement only.
  • Malware & exfiltration analysisStatic and dynamic analysis of commodity RATs and stealers: C2 protocol reconstruction, payload extraction, IOC derivation.
  • Data toolingCollectors, change-detection monitors and public datasets with documented provenance and reproducible builds.
  • Application securityAttack-surface analysis of Android and web applications: exported components, authorization boundaries, cross-app data flows.
